## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an ultra-refined, editorial commerce sensibility rooted in understated luxury, restraint, and deliberate curation. Drawing inspiration from high-fashion maisons and architectural monographs, the aesthetic balances visceral tactility with severe minimalism.

- **Personality**: Monastic luxury, discerning, serene, architectural, and timeless.
- **Audience**: Cultivated collectors, architectural patrons, and high-net-worth design enthusiasts seeking deliberate objects over disposable volume.
- **Emotional Resonance**: An atmosphere of hushed prestige, calm precision, and museum-grade reverence.
- **Design Movement**: Elevated Editorial Minimalism paired with Architectural Grid Discipline. We eschew gratuitous digital artifacts (heavy blurs, artificial drop shadows, garish gradients) in favor of calibrated negative space, razor-sharp hairline dividers, disciplined typography, and warm earthen tonalities.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in tactile, earth-derived neutrals that avoid the stark sterility of pure digital white and jet black.

- **Primary Background (`#F5F1E9` Warm Ivory)**: The default canvas tone. Provides a rich, gallery-like foundation with high warmth and organic softness.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#D8CBB8` Sand Beige & `#FFFFFF` Soft White)**: Applied selectively for distinct container blocks, product display plinths, and elevated drawers.
- **Primary Type & Structure (`#22221F` Obsidian Black)**: High-contrast ink tone for headlines, body typography, structural borders, and key interactive touchpoints. Never replace with `#000000`.
- **Accent & Highlight (`#A18A70` Muted Bronze)**: Reserved for understated metadata, subtle hover states, focus indicators, and bespoke badge borders.
- **Micro-Borders (`rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.08)`)**: Structural razor hairlines that establish spatial tension and section cadence without visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic haute-couture editorial presence and modern technical clarity.

- **Headlines & Display**: Playfair Display introduces literary grandeur. Headlines lean into generous line heights and subtle negative tracking at large scales to preserve visual density.
- **Body & Functional Text**: Plus Jakarta Sans provides high legibility, neutral geometry, and humanistic warmth. It anchors technical specs, descriptions, and user inputs without competing with display elements.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: All navigational triggers, collection classifications, status markers, and operational metadata must be styled in uppercase with extreme tracking (`0.18em` to `0.22em`) for an archival monograph feel.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts favor asymmetry, structural pacing, and extensive negative space over uniform grid-density.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column architectural grid on desktop; 4-column structure on mobile. Column alignments frequently embrace asymmetric offsets—such as pairing an editorial text block spanning 4 columns alongside a 7-column imagery reveal with 1-column negative gutters.
- **Canvas Margins**: Deep canvas boundaries (`margin: 4rem` on desktop) frame the experience like a heavy mat around a fine art photograph.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace**: Generous spacing tiers (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) separate product categories and curated narratives, preventing the psychological pressure of discount-driven retail interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation is created strictly through surface tinting and calibrated hairlines, entirely rejecting conventional dropshadows.

- **Planar Tiers**: Depth is expressed through contrasting tonal planes:
  - Base: `#F5F1E9` Warm Ivory.
  - Plinths & Interactive Panels: `#FFFFFF` Soft White or `#D8CBB8` Sand Beige.
  - Overlays / Drawers: `#F5F1E9` Warm Ivory bordered with crisp `1px solid rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.12)`.
- **Borders over Shadows**: Spatial contours and floating elements utilize 1px hairline dividers (`rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.08)` or `#A18A70` Muted Bronze for accents).
- **Dimming Modals**: Full-screen dialogs and cart drawers introduce a calm scrim tinted with Obsidian Black at low opacity (`rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.35)`), maintaining visible background architecture.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular is strictly sharp-edged (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Architectural Edge**: All containers, interactive components, modals, inputs, and image frames must retain crisp `0px` border radii.
- **Visual Tension**: Zero roundedness reinforces the tactile precision of stone, metal, and heavy cardstock found in luxury physical packaging.
- **Imagery**: Photography is strictly rectangular or square, treated with bleed margins and framing lines rather than soft corner clips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#22221F` Obsidian Black background, `#F5F1E9` Warm Ivory uppercase text (`label-caps-md`), 0px radius, padding `1rem 2.5rem`. Hover state transitions background smoothly to `#A18A70` Muted Bronze over 300ms.
- **Secondary (Ghost)**: Transparent background, 1px perimeter border in `#22221F`, Obsidian Black uppercase text. Hover state inverts to solid `#22221F` with `#F5F1E9` text.
- **Editorial Text Link**: Inline uppercase label with an active 1px underline positioned 4px below text, rendering in `#A18A70`. Underline extends or contracts upon hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges**: Background transparent or `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px `rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.15)`. Text styled using `label-caps-sm` in `#22221F`. No rounded corners.
- **Selection Chips**: Minimal tab-style rectangles with `1px solid rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.1)`. Selected state fills container with `#22221F` and flips label to `#F5F1E9`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Unbordered or framed with faint 1px hairline dividers (`rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.08)`).
- **Image Treatment**: Generous 4:5 or 3:4 portrait ratios, sitting directly on `#FFFFFF` or `#D8CBB8` surface plinths. Hover initiates an imperceptible scale shift (e.g., `scale(1.02)`) over 700ms ease-out.
- **Card Meta**: Title rendered in `headline-sm`, pricing and collection metadata set in `body-sm` and `label-caps-sm`. Never clutter with persistent badges or noisy discount callouts.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs**: Flat bottom-border style (1px `rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.2)`) or minimal sharp box with `#FFFFFF` background. No rounded corners.
- **Typographic Treatment**: Labels in `label-caps-sm` hovering crisply above inputs. Input text set in `body-md` in `#22221F`. Focus state converts bottom border to `#22221F` without heavy outlines or glow effects.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: Precise 16px square boxes, 1px border `#22221F`. Checked state fills with `#22221F` containing a sharp, hairline Warm Ivory tick.
- **Radio Buttons**: Sharp-cornered diamond markers or structural rectangular segmented pills that toggle active state via solid fill instead of bubble radios.

### Cart Drawer & Slide-overs
- **Flyout Canvas**: Slides in from screen edge using `#F5F1E9` background, separated from content with 1px border in `rgba(34, 34, 31, 0.1)`.
- **Order Summary**: Hairline separators isolate totals and tax lines. Primary checkout triggers span full width with commanding Obsidian presence.
